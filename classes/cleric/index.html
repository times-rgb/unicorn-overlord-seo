Cleric Guide: Stats, Promotion & Counters | Unicorn Overlord

Cleric is the primary healer class: its Row Heal keeps frontline guard thresholds alive and it promotes to Bishop for stronger holy magic. Low offense means it needs initiative gear to heal before the enemy acts.

Class Promotion & Unlock Requirements

  • Promoted Class: Bishop
  • Promotion Cost: 30 Honors, Medal Level 2
  • Key Gains: AP +1, PP +1, Holy magic access and an accessory slot

Class Matchups & Counter Synergy

  • Strong against: attrition fights — sustain outlasts chip damage from Warrior and archers.
  • Weak against: burst damage and assassins that can one-round the back line before a heal lands.
  • Best synergy: Scarlett as the premium Cleric, fronted by Alain sustain.

Best Characters for Cleric

  • Scarlett: best-in-class Priestess → High Priestess with strong row heals.
  • Equip Crimson Pendant to out-speed enemy damage dealers.
