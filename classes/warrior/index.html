Warrior Guide: Stats, Promotion & Counters | Unicorn Overlord

Warrior is the physical guard-break specialist: deploy it to erase armored frontliners and open a damage lane for cavalry. Its high Physical Attack trades speed and magical resilience for reliable armor pressure.

Class Matchups & Counter Synergy

  • Strong against: Hoplite, Legionnaire—guard break converts their defense into a liability.
  • Weak against: Wyvern Knight, Wizard—evasion and magic bypass the intended target profile.
  • Best synergy: Alain supplies sustain while Warriors remove protected targets.

Best Characters for Warrior

  • Berenice: high Attack scaling makes early armor checks predictable.
  • Alain: pairs a frontline aura with a breaker slot.
